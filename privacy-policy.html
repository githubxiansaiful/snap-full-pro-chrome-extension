Privacy Policy for SnapFull

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Single Purpose & Local Execution

SnapFull is a Chrome Extension designed exclusively to capture full webpage screenshots, visible viewports, custom regions, and DOM elements, with local editing and annotation tools. All processing occurs 100% locally on your computer.

2. No Data Collection or Transmission

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are zero analytics scripts, trackers, or telemetries.
  • No External Servers: The extension operates entirely offline without external communication.

3. Permissions & Justifications

  • activeTab: Allows the extension to interact with the active page to measure dimensions and capture screenshots when clicked.
  • scripting: Injects temporary page-scrolling helpers and region crop overlays during capture.
  • storage & unlimitedStorage: Stores your local settings and local thumbnail history in browser memory/IndexedDB.
  • downloads: Saves exported images (PNG, JPEG, PDF) to your local Downloads folder.
  • tabs: Opens the full-screen Studio Annotation Editor tab.

4. Third-Party Services

SnapFull contains zero external dependencies, CDNs, or tracking services.

5. Contact

For questions or support regarding SnapFull, please reach out via our GitHub repository or extension support channel.
